Claim your listing

Claiming your business lets you set your own ownership self-identification, hours, contact details, and photos. It requires at least two matching verification factors — this keeps the "Arab-owned" and "claimed & verified" badges meaningful.

  1. 1

    Find your listing

    Search by city and category, or tell us the business name below.

  2. 2

    Verify by email

    We send a link to an email address at the business's own domain, matching the listed website.

  3. 3

    Verify by phone

    We send a one-time code by SMS or automated voice call to the listed phone number — only after you request it, never unsolicited.

  4. 4

    Or verify by mail

    No phone or domain email on file? We can mail a postcard with a 6-digit PIN to the business address.

  5. 5

    Two factors confirm your claim

    Once two matching factors are verified, your listing status changes to "claimed & verified" and you can edit it directly.
